01 — Our commitment

## A structured framework for every complaint

Etribe Finance Private Limited has established a grievance redressal mechanism to handle customer concerns promptly and fairly. We treat every complaint as a chance to improve the way we serve you. Our framework rests on five commitments:

### Easy access

Multiple channels to lodge a complaint, so raising a concern is never the hard part.

### Customer protection

Your interests are protected throughout the resolution process, across every product we offer.

### Transparency & defined timelines

A clear Turn-Around-Time at every level, and honest communication if a complaint can’t be resolved in time.

### A clear escalation path

If you’re not satisfied with a resolution, a defined matrix lets you escalate to higher authorities.

### Learning from feedback

We use complaints to keep improving our processes and products to match what you need.

03 — How to raise it

## Lodge a complaint

Email is our primary channel. To help us resolve your complaint faster and in full, please include the details listed below, ideally from your registered email ID.

Channels

#### Email us

Write to [help@prosparity.in](mailto:help@prosparity.in) from your registered email ID.

What to include in your complaint

Your name, address and contact details (email, phone / mobile).

Relationship information such as your Application ID.

Reference number of a previous complaint or request, if any.

Details and the nature of the complaint.

Copies of supporting documents, wherever applicable.

What happens after you raise it

Your complaint is acknowledged when an employee receives it.

We work to resolve it within the Turn-Around-Time shown in the escalation matrix.

Once the investigation is complete, we share a detailed response with any supporting documents.

#### Submit a complaint

Prefer a form? Fill this in and we’ll route it to our support team. You can also email [help@prosparity.in](mailto:help@prosparity.in) directly.

Full name

Application ID (optional)

Email

Phone / mobile

Nature of complaint

Previous complaint ref. (if any)

Details of your complaint

By submitting, you agree we may contact you about this complaint. Supporting documents can be emailed to help@prosparity.in with your reference.

05 — Regulatory channels

## RBI portals

The Reserve Bank of India provides dedicated portals for reporting and escalating grievances against regulated entities.

RBI Sachet

#### Report unauthorised entities

Lodge complaints and access information about entities that collect deposits or operate without authorisation.

sachet.rbi.org.in

RBI CMS

#### Complaint Management System

File a complaint with the RBI Ombudsman after exhausting all internal escalation levels.

cms.rbi.org.in

When to approach
  If your complaint is not resolved within 30 days, or you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.
