## 01

## Applicability

These Cancellation and Refund Terms are applicable to all types of digital payments made to or through Etribe Finance Private Limited.

<!-- section 4 -->

## 02

## Failed Transactions

- If an amount is debited from the customer's bank account, card, or wallet despite a failed transaction due to any reason, the onus shall be on the customer's bank, card issuer, or wallet provider to reverse the amount.

<!-- section 5 -->

## 03

## Duplicate or Excess Payments

- In the event that a customer has attempted to make an online payment more than once and Etribe Finance Private Limited has received the amount more than once, Etribe Finance Private Limited reserves the right to adjust such excess amount against additional EMI(s) towards the customer's loan account.
- If Etribe Finance Private Limited decides to refund the excess amount, it shall be processed through electronic mode within one (1) month from the date of claim acceptance.
- In the case of a customer holding more than one loan account, Etribe Finance Private Limited reserves the right to adjust the excess amount towards any one or more account(s) as it deems fit, irrespective of the bifurcation provided by the customer.

<!-- section 6 -->

## 04

## Refund Process

- The customer must submit a written application or send an email to Etribe Finance Private Limited requesting a refund, along with the transaction number and original payment receipt (if any) generated at the time of making the payment.
- The customer must claim any refund within seven (7) days of the date of payment.

<!-- section 7 -->

## 05

## Limitation of Liability

Etribe Finance Private Limited assumes no responsibility and shall incur no liability if it is unable to effect any Payment Instruction(s) on the Payment Date owing to any one or more of the following circumstances:

- The Payment Instruction(s) issued by the customer is/are incomplete, inaccurate, invalid, or delayed.
- The Payment Account has insufficient funds or limits to cover the amount as mentioned in the Payment Instruction(s).
- The funds available in the Payment Account are under any encumbrance or charge.
- The customer's bank refuses or delays honouring the Payment Instruction(s).
- Circumstances beyond the control of the Company, including but not limited to fire, flood, natural disasters, bank strikes, power failure, or systems failure such as computer or telephone line breakdown due to an unforeseeable cause or interference from an outside force.
- In the event the payment is not processed for any reason, the customer shall be intimated about the failed payment via email.

<!-- section 8 -->

## 06

## Suspension of Online Payment Services

- Etribe Finance Private Limited may, in its sole discretion and for any or no reason, suspend or terminate the use of online payment services from any payment gateway service provider.
