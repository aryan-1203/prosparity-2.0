## Our collections platform is built in-house, not licensed.

96.4%

Collection efficiency

[link] Inside the stack -> /technology

01

### Sourcing

Dealer-facing app

Lead-to-sanction for RMs and dealer staff — OTP, KYC, house-ownership declaration, product selection, offer. Built for patchy connectivity.

02

### Credit

Proprietary scoring

Two models: one scores customers on bureau plus field-collected signals; one scores dealers and OEMs before onboarding. Dealer quality is our strongest predictor of portfolio quality.

03

### Core

LOS / LMS integrations

Origination and loan management with bureau, KYC, e-sign, e-NACH and payments. Partner-wise books report each lending structure separately.
