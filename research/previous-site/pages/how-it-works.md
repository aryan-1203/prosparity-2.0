### Lending partner to ProsParity

- Partner lenders and NBFCs supply capital under 100–0, 80–20 co-lending, or term-debt structures. We originate, underwrite and service each loan for its full life.
- 02
- Physical

### Walk-in at the dealership

- Most loans start when a customer visits a partner dealership — the dealer is part of the credit decision.
- 03
- Digital

### Dealer-facing app

- Sourcing runs on our own app, operated by our sales team or dealers. No paper forms.
- 04
- Digital

### Digital underwriting

- Bureau, KYC and verification run while the customer is still at the dealership.
- 05
- Physical

### Field assessment

- A field officer visits home or workplace — how we see thin-file borrowers bureaus can't.
- 06
- Digital

### Loan disbursed

- Documentation is completed digitally and the repayment mandate is set up before funds are released.
- 07
- Physical
- Our own tele-calling and field teams, on a platform we built — from allocation to legal. Never outsourced.
