If you have a concern about a loan, a payment, or how you’ve been treated, this page tells you exactly where to raise it and what happens next, at every level, up to the RBI Ombudsman.

Operated by Etribe Finance Private Limited

<!-- section 4 -->

## 02

## A structured framework for every complaint

Etribe Finance Private Limited has established a grievance redressal mechanism to handle customer concerns promptly and fairly. We treat every complaint as a chance to improve the way we serve you. Our framework rests on five commitments:

- Easy access: Multiple channels to lodge a complaint, so raising a concern is never the hard part.
- Customer protection: Your interests are protected throughout the resolution process, across every product we offer.
- Transparency & defined timelines: A clear Turn-Around-Time at every level, and honest communication if a complaint can’t be resolved in time.
- A clear escalation path: If you’re not satisfied with a resolution, a defined matrix lets you escalate to higher authorities.
- Learning from feedback: We use complaints to keep improving our processes and products to match what you need.

Start at Level 1. If your concern isn’t resolved to your satisfaction, escalate to the next level. You can approach the RBI Ombudsman only after exhausting all internal levels.

- Level 1: Raise your complaint through the channels below and our team takes it from there.
- Level 2: Not satisfied with the Level 1 outcome, or it’s still unresolved? Escalate here.
- Level 3: If you’re still not satisfied with the resolution provided, escalate to our Business Head.
- RBI Ombudsman: If your grievance remains unresolved after exhausting all internal levels, you may approach the RBI Ombudsman through the CMS portal.

## 04

## Lodge a complaint

Email is our primary channel. To help us resolve your complaint faster and in full, please include the details listed below, ideally from your registered email ID.

Email us: Write to help@prosparity.in from your registered email ID.

What to include in your complaint

- Your name, address and contact details (email, phone / mobile).
- Relationship information such as your Application ID.
- Reference number of a previous complaint or request, if any.
- Details and the nature of the complaint.
- Copies of supporting documents, wherever applicable.

What happens after you raise it

- Your complaint is acknowledged when an employee receives it.
- We work to resolve it within the Turn-Around-Time shown in the escalation matrix.
- Once the investigation is complete, we share a detailed response with any supporting documents.

## 06

## RBI portals

The Reserve Bank of India provides dedicated portals for reporting and escalating grievances against regulated entities.

- RBI Sachet — Report unauthorised entities: Lodge complaints and access information about entities that collect deposits or operate without authorisation. https://sachet.rbi.org.in/
- RBI CMS — Complaint Management System: File a complaint with the RBI Ombudsman after exhausting all internal escalation levels. https://cms.rbi.org.in/cms/indexpage.html#eng

[dt] When to approach

[dd] If your complaint is not resolved within 30 days, or you are not satisfied with the resolution, you may approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.
